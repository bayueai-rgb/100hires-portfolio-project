# Cold Outreach Playbook Outline — Derived from Research

## Phase 1: ICP & Signal Identification
- Framework: Golden Path (30MPC) + Signal-Based Prospecting (Jason Bay)
- Tools: LinkedIn Sales Navigator, intent data platforms, Clay

## Phase 2: Message Crafting
- Cold Email: 3C Framework (Alex Berman)
- Cold Call: Permission-Based Opener + The Ledge (30MPC)
- LinkedIn: Pattern Interrupt + Anti-Template (Belal Batrawy)

## Phase 3: Execution & Sequencing
- Multi-channel: LinkedIn + Email + Call (Jack Reamer / SalesBread)
- Deliverability: Subject line A/B testing, CTA structure (Jeremy Chatelaine / QuickMail)
- Enterprise: Multi-threading buying committees (Jen Allen-Knuth)

## Phase 4: Optimization
- Performance-based: Pay-per-meeting model as quality benchmark (Nick Abraham / Leadbird)
- AI Integration: Clay + AI personalization (Vincent Fourcade / ColdIQ)
- Buyer Perspective: What actually gets ignored and why (Vin Matano)

## Key Metrics to Track Positive reply rate (benchmark: SalesBread achieves 48.14%)
- Meeting booking rate per sequence
- Cost per qualified lead
- Pipeline velocity by channel
