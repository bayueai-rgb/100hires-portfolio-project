Status: Transcript unavailable. Summary from Jason Bay's frameworks.

## Detailed Summary
Jason Bay (trainer for Zoom, Gong) argues volume-based outreach is dead.
- Signal-Based Personalization: Use real buying signals (job changes, funding, hiring, tech adoption)
- Martini Glass Funnel: Wide (signals) -> Narrow (disqualify) -> Wide (multi-thread)
- Disqualification-First: Fast "no" on bad fits = more time for quality
- Tools: LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Crunchbase, BuiltWith
Key message: In an AI-flooded inbox, only genuine relevance stands out.
