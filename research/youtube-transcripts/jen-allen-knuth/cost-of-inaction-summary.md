## Core Framework: Cost of Inaction (COI)

Most reps sell ROI. Top reps sell **COI** — what happens if the prospect does NOTHING.

### Why COI > ROI
- ROI is speculative ("You could save 20%")
- COI is concrete ("You're already losing $50K/month to manual processes")
- COI creates urgency. ROI creates interest.

### The COI Formula
1. **Quantify current pain** — "How many hours does your team spend on X?"
2. **Annualize it** — "So that's 2,000 hours/year at $75/hour = $150K"
3. **Add risk** — "Plus the 3 deals you lost last quarter due to delayed response"
4. **Compare to solution cost** — "This costs $30K/year"

## Enterprise Application
Jen trained teams at G2, GE, IBM. Previously closed 7-ls at Challenger.
- In enterprise, **consensus-building** is the real sale
- COI helps you create internal champions who can justify the purchase to CFO/CEO
- Multi-threading: Never rely on one stakeholder

## Why This Is Critical for B2B SaaS
SMB buyers want ROI. Enterprise buyers need COI to defend the budget. If you only have ROI content, you can't sell upmarket.
