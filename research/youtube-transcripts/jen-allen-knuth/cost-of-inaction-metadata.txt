In this Metrics & Chill interview we hear from Jen Allen-Knuth, founder of DemandJen, which delivers SKO keynotes & virtual workshops to help Sales teams defeat customer status quo and earn buyer attention. We  discussed the concept of Cost of Inaction (COI) and its significance in sales and marketing. She emphasizes the importance of understanding COI as a metric that highlights the negative consequences of not taking action, contrasting it with traditional ROI metrics. Jen outlines a framework for identifying COI, stressing the need for empathy in sales conversations and the importance of aligning messaging with customer goals. She also addresses common pitfalls companies face when implementing COI strategies and the need to recognize status quo losses. The discussion concludes with insights on measuring the impact of COI and integrating it across various business functions.
