## Top 3 Frameworks Extracted

### 1. Permission-Based Opener (PBO)
Instead of "Did I catch you at a bad time?" → "I heard your name tossed around, mind if I tell you why?"  
**Why it works:** Transfers control to the prospect. Reduces hang-up rate by 40%+.

### 2. The Ledge
When prospect says "Not interested" → "I completely understand. Before I let you go — what specifically isn't interesting?"  
**Why it works:** Forces specificity. Turns brush-offs into real objections you can solve.

### 3. Golden Path Sequencing
Map 5 common triggers (hiring, funding, expansion, new leadership, tech change) to specific outreach sequences.  
**Why it works:** Trigger-based outreach gets 3-5x replies vs. generic cadencesKey Data Points
