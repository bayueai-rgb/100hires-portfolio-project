# 3C Framework (Alex Berman)
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9Rl_QUTPLA
Status: Transcript unavailable. Summary from Alex Berman's published work.

## Detailed Summary
Alex Berman (1M+ cold emails sent) breaks down his 3C Framework:
1. Compliment — Specific and genuine. "Loved your article on X" not "Great profile."
2. Case Study — Relevant result. "We helped [similar company] book 47 meetings/month."
3. Clear CTA — One low-friction ask. "15-min call next Tuesday at 10am?"
Designed to be reproducible by SDR teams at scale for B2B SaaS.
