# Detailed Key Takeaways: The 3C Cold Email Framework (Alex Berman)

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9Rl_QUTPLA
Collected: June 2026

## 3C Framework
1. Compliment → Personalized & specific
2. Case Study → Relevant social proof
3. Clear CTA → One low-friction call-to-action

Alex Berman has sent over 1 million cold emails and generated hundreds of thousands of meetings. Very actionable framework.
