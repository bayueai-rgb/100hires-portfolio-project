## Detailed Summary
How to identify and act on buying signals:
- Top signals: Job changes, funding announcements, new job postings, tech stack changes, LinkedIn activity spikes
- Tools: LinkedIn filteunchbase alerts, BuiltWith
- Speed matters: Contact within 48 hours of signal = significantly higher conversion
- Integrate signals into 3C Framework (use in Compliment section)
Core message: Let real-world triggers tell you who to email.
