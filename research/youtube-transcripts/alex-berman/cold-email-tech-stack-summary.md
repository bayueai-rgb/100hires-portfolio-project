## Core Framework: 3C + Tech Stack

### The 3C Email Structure
1. **Compliment** — Specific, researched (e.g., "Saw you expanded to APAC last quarter")
2. **Case Study** — Relevant result in same industry/vertical
3. **CTA** — One low-friction ask ("Worth a 2-min conversation?")

### Tech Stack Revealed
- **Lead Gen:** Apollo.io + LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- **Enrichment:** Clay + Clearbit
- **Email:** Instantly / Smartlead (for deliverability)
- **Tracking:** Open rates matter less than reply rates

## Key Tactical Insights
- Alex sent 1M+ emails; 80% of meetings come from follow-ups 3-7
- First line determines everything — spend 50% of writing time there
- Subject line: Curiosity gap > Summary (e.g., "Qion" beats "About your SaaS growth")

## Why This Matters for Demand Gen
Reproducible, scalable system. Cost per meeting drops to $15-30 when sequenced correctly.
