## Core Thesis: The Top 20% Rule

Cold calling isn't dead — **bad cold calling** is dead. AI has raised the bar. Only the top 20% of reps will survive.

### The New Standard: Signal-Based + Hyper-Personalized
- Old way: 100 dials/day with generic script
- New way: 20 dials/day with researched, signal-based opens

### Key Frameworks
1. **Martini Glass Funnel** — Wide top (many signals), narrow bottom (only high-fit)
2. **Disqualification-First** — Filter out bad fits in first 30 seconds to save time
3. **Precision Over Volume** — 10 hyper-personalized calls beat 100 generic ones

## Practical Application
Jason trains teams at Zoom, Gong, Monday.com. His methodology:
- Use LinkedIn + job boards + fund for pre-call research
- AI for research, human for delivery
- Goal: Book meeting, not pitch product

## Strategic Insight for SaaS
In APAC markets, cultural nuance makes signal-based calling even more critical. Generic Western scripts fail.
