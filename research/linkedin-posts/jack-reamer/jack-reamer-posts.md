Our LinkedIn outreach has a 48% positive reply rate. The exact sequence:

Day 1: Connection request (no note)
Day 3: Voice message after they accept (30 seconds, role-specific)
Day 5: Text message referencing their recent post
Day 8: Share relevant content (no ask)
Day 12: Soft ask - Would this be relevant to what you are building at [Company]?

5 touches before you ask for anything. That is the rule.

Ultra-personalization does not mean mentioning their name 3 times.

WEAK: Hi Sarah, I noticed you are the VP of Sales at Acme Corp.
STRONG: Hi Sarah, your comment on the SaaStr thread about SDR-to-AE handoff ratios was the most practical take I have seen on that problem.

One shows you know their company. One shows you read what they wrote. Reply rate difference: 6% vs 34%.

50 targeted LinkedIn outreaches per day
