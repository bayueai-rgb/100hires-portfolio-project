## Post 1: The Permission-Based Opener That Changed Everything
*Date: 2026-06-10*

Most cold calls fail in the first 15 seconds. Here's why:

❌ "Hi this is Armand from X company, how are you today?"
→ Screams "sales call." Instant wall.

✅ "Hey Sarah, I heard your name come up in a conversation with Mark from Acme. He mentioned you're scaling the outbound team. Mind if I take 30 seconds to explain why I'm calling?"

Why this works:
- Name drops create instant credibility
- "30 seconds" gives them control
- You're not selling yet — you're connecting

This is the Permission-Based Opener. Armand and Nick break it down in detail on 30 Minutes to President's Club.

The goal of a cold call isn't to close. It's to earn the next conversation.

## Post 2: Why Generic Sequences Are Dead
*Date: 2026-06-05yzed 10,000+ cold outreach sequences. The finding:

**Spray-and-pray reply rate:** 1-3%
**Trigger-based sequence reply rate:** 12-18%

The difference? Trigger-based sequencing.

Here's how we do it at 30MPC:

1. Identify the 5 most common triggers for your ICP (funding, hiring, tool adoption, etc.)
2. Build ONE "Golden Path" sequence per trigger
3. Each sequence has custom opener + relevant case study + specific CTA

Example for "just raised Series A" trigger:
- Opener: "Congrats on the raise. Most Series A founders I talk to start building outbound at this stage..."
- Case Study: "We helped [similar company] go from 0 to 50 meetings/month in 90 days after their A."
- CTA: "Worth 15 minutes to see if the playbook fits?"

One size fits none. Triggers fit all.
