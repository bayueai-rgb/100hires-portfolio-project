Post 1 | Date: 2024-11-12 | Likes: 4,200

The single biggest mistake in cold calls: asking How are you at the start.

Gong data shows reps who open with How are you have 10% lower connect-to-meeting rate.

Instead, use the Permission-Based Opener (PBO):
Hey [Name], I know I'm catching you out of the blue - got 30 seconds for me to share why I called?

Why it works: you acknowledge the interruption, ask permission, set a time expectation. We tracked this across 400+ reps. PBO outperforms every time.

Nobody taught you The Ledge and it's costing you pipeline.

When a prospect says I'm not interested, most reps fold. Top reps use The Ledge:

That's totally fair - most people I talk to say the same thing before they hear what I have. All I'm asking for is 27 seconds. If it doesn't land, I'll let you go.

The goal isn't to overcome the objection. It's to buy enough time to make it irrelevant.

Post 3 | Date: 2024-09-15 | Likes: 5,100

The Golden Path framework:

Step 1: Find your top 5 trigger events (job change, funding, hiring surge, tech adoption, competitor switch)
Step 2: Map each trigger to your best-performing sequence
Step 3: Never send a generic sequence again

We analyzed 50,000 sequences. Trigger-based outreach has 3.2x higher reply rates than generic cadences. Stop spraying. Start triggering.

Post 4 | Date: 2024-08-20 | Likes: 2,900

The Reply Method: why Are you open to a call kills your reply rate.

Bad CTA: Would you be open to a 15-minute call next week?
Good CTA: Is reducing ramp time for new AEs something on your radar for Q4?

Interest-based CTAs outperform meeting requests by 40%. The ask should feel like a question, not a trap.

Post 5 | Date: 2024-07-30 | Likes: 3,400

We interviewed 495 top sellers for the podcast. The number 1 thing the best ones have in common: they never pitch on the first cold call. They diagnose.

What does your current onboarding look like for new reps?
How long does it take a new AE to hit quota?
What happens if they do not hit it?

By the time the prospect answers these 3 questions, they have sold themselves.
