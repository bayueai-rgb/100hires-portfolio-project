We only charge for meetings booked. Here is what that forces us to do:

1. Ruthless ICP definition - cannot afford to target anyone who will not buy
2. Obsessive deliverability - one spam flag kills our economics
3. Message quality over volume - 50 great emails beat 500 generic ones
4. Pre-qualification before booking - we do not book meetings that will not close

Most agencies charge for sends. We charge for outcomes. That model change forces every other decision.

Post 3 | Date: 2024-08-14 | Likes: 1,600

A meeting-ready lead has 4 characteristics:

1. Budget authority - they can actually say yes
2. Active problem - experiencing the pain now, not theoretically
3. Timeframe - needs a solution in next 90 days
4. Awareness - knows their current approach is not working

Most outreach tries to create all 4. The best outreach finds accounts where all 4 already exist. Research first. Outreach second. Always.
