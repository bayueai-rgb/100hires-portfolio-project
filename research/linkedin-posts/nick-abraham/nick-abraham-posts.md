## Post 1: Why We Only Get Paid for Meetings, Not Emails
*Date: 2026-06-11*
At Leadbird, clients only pay for meeting-ready leads. No retainer, no fluff. This forces extreme discipline: we can't afford generic emails. Every campaign must be dialed in on ICP, messaging, timing. If the lead isn't qualified, we don't get paid. Ask your outbound team: "What happens if we don't book a meeting this month?" If the answer isn't clear, you're paying for activity, not results.

## Post 2: The Meeting-Ready Definition Most Teams Get Wrong
*Date: 2026-06-04*
A meeting-ready leadt pass 3 filters: 1) Fit – matches ICP? 2) Intent – trigger event? 3) Authority – can influence purchase? I've seen teams celebrate 20 meetings/month only to find half were with interns. Fix: define "meeting-ready" with your sales team before any campaign. Align on what a good meeting looks like, not just a booked slot.
