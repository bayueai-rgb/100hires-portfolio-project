## Post 1: Deliverability Is the Silent Killer of Cold Email
*Date: 2026-06-10*
Best copy in the world doesn't matter if it lands in spam. At QuickMail, #1 reason campaigns fail isn't bad messaging — it's deliverability. 3 rules: 1) Warm up new domains over 4-6 weeks. 2) Use custom tracking domains (shared links burn reputation). 3) Keep bounce rates under 3%. Technical setup matters as much as creative.
#coldemail #deliverability

## Post 2: Why We A/B Test Subject Lines Obsessively
*Date: 2026-06-03*
Thousands of tests at QuickMail: short subject lines (3-5 words) beat lCuriosity beats clarity — "Quick question" outperforms "Here's how we can help." No caps, no spam words. One of the best? Just the prospect's company name. Simple, personal, cuts through noise. A 10% open rate lift = dozens more meetings/month at scale.
#emailmarketing #abtesting
