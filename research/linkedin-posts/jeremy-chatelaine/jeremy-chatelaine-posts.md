After analyzing 50M+ cold emails through QuickMail:

Subject lines under 5 words: 28% open rate
Subject lines over 10 words: 14% open rate

Emails under 75 words: highest reply rates
Emails over 200 words: reply rates drop 60%

Yes/No question CTAs: 2x reply rate vs open-ended
Single CTA: 40% higher reply rate than multiple options

Data beats intuition. Every time.

The deliverability checklist nobody talks about:

Technical setup (non-negotiable):
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC all configured
- Custom tracking domain
- Domain age: 90+ days before cold outreach
- Warmup: 4+ weeks at 20-30 emails per day

Sending behavior:
- Max 30-50 emails per day per inbox
- Random delays between sends
- Unsubscribe link in every email

If your emails are not landing in inbox, your copywriting does not matter. Fix the foundation first.
