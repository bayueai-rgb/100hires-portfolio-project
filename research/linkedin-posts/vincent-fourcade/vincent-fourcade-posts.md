## Post 2: The SDR Role Isn't Dying. It's Evolving.
*Date: 2026-06-02*

I keep seeing posts: "AI will replace SDRs by 2027."

Wrong framing.

The SDR role isn't dying. It's splitting:

**SDR 1.0 (dying):**
- Manual list building
- 100 calls/day
- Generic scripts
- "Smile and dial"

**SDR 2.0 (emerging):**
- AI-powered lead enrichment
- Signal-based prioritization
- Hyper-personalized messaging
- 40 high-intent touches/day > 200 random calls

The skills are changing. But the human element — reading a room, handling objections, building rapport — that's not getting automated.

At ColdIQ, our SDRs book 3x more meetings than industry average. Not because they work harder. Because they work smarter with AI.

The future belongs to SDRs who embrace the tools. Not those who fear them.

What's your take? Is the SDR role dead or evolving?
