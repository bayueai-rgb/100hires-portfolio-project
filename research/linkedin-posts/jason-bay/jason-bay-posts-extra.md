## Post 1: AI Killed Generic Outreach. Good.
*Date: 2026-06-12*

Unpopular opinion: ChatGPT didn't ruin cold outreach — it exposed who was doing it wrong.

Before AI:
- Spray 1000 generic emails → 20 replies → 5 meetings
- "Volume covers my bad messaging"

After AI:
- Every inbox is flooded with AI-generated fluff
- Generic emails now get 0.5% reply rates
- Volume can't save you anymore

The solution: **Signal-based personalization.**

Instead of "I saw you work at X company..."
→ "I noticed you're hiring 3 SDRs this quarter. Most teams scaling at that pace struggle with ramp time. We cut it by 40%."

That's a signal. That's personal. That's human.

AI didn't kill outreach. It raised the bar.

## Post 2: The Martini Glass Funnel
*Date: 2026-06-03*

Most outbound funnels are shaped like a megaphone:
Wide at top (lots of leadw at bottom (few meetings)

But the best outbound teams? They use the **Martini Glass Funnel:**

WIDE → NARROW → WIDE

1. **Wide top:** Start with many signals and triggers
2. **Narrow middle:** Aggressively disqualify. If they're not a fit, move on FAST.
3. **Wide bottom:** Multi-thread into the account. One champion isn't enough.

The insight: Disqualification is a skill. The faster you say "no" to bad fits, the more time you have for real opportunities.

How fast do you disqualify?
