- Deep at base: go extremely deep on 20-30 accounts per month

20 deeply researched prospects beats 200 generic ones. Every time.

Post 3 | Date: 2024-09-22 | Likes: 1,900

Disqualification-first prospecting changed my close rate.

My 4-filter checklist:
1. Do they have budget authority?
2. Is there a trigger event in last 90 days?
3. Are they using a competitor or DIY solution?
4. Is the company in a growth phase?

If a prospect fails 2 of 4: remove them. Result: fewer conversations, higher close rates.

Post 4 | Date: 2024-08-08 | Likes: 2,300

Stop writing cold emails. Start writing business cases.

Cold email: We help companies like yours increase revenue.
Business case: Companies in your segment with 50-200 reps typically leave $400K in pipeline annually from poor follow-up sequencing.

One is about you. One is about them. Specificity is the new personalization.
