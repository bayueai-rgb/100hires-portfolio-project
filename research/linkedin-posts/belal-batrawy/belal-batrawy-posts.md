## Post 1: The Mic Drop Method
*Date: 2026-06-07*

Most cold emails sound like this:
"I hope this email finds you well. I wanted to introduce our AI-powered platform that helps companies like yours..."

🤮 Fluff. Delete. Next.

Here's what I use instead — the **Mic Drop Method:**

Open with one bold, undeniable statement that makes them STOP scrolling.

Examples:
- "Your last 3 SDR hires probably won't hit quota in 6 months."
- "Most Series B companies waste 40% of their outbound budget on bad data."
- "Your cold email reply rate is about to get worse. Here's why."

No greeting. No "I hope this finds you well." No fluff.

Just a pattern interrupt.

Then: Back it up with data. Then: Bridge to your solution.

That's it. That's the method.

The first sentence decides if they read the second. Make it count.

## Post 2: C Can't Write This
*Date: 2026-05-30*

Hot take: AI can write a "good enough" cold email.

But "good enough" gets deleted.

Great cold emails require:
1. **Psychology** — What is the prospect actually worried about right now?
2. **Timing** — Why today? What trigger makes this urgent?
3. **Voice** — Does it sound human, or like a robot wrote it?

AI gives you the skeleton. You need to add the soul.

Here's my challenge: Write one cold email this week WITHOUT using ChatGPT. Just you, a blank page, and what you know about the prospect.

You might surprise yourself.

Try it. Then tell me how it felt.
