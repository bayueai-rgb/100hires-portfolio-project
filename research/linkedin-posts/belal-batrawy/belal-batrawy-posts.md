Most cold emails fail in the first 5 words.

I hope this email finds you - DELETE
My name is X and I - DELETE
We help companies like yours - DELETE

The pattern interrupt that works: lead with the problem, not yourself.

[Their competitor] just raised $50M. Your team has 6 months before they outspend you on [category].

Now they are reading. Because it is about them, not you.

Post 2 | Date: 2024-10-19 | Likes: 4,200

The Mic Drop Method for cold calls:

Drop a provocative insight and go silent.

Most VPs of Sales I talk to tell me their biggest problem is not closing - it is that their pipeline is full of deals that should never have been created. Does that resonate, or are you in a different situation?

Then. Stop. Talking. The silence is the close. Most reps fill it with more pitch. Let the silence work.

Post 3 | Date: 2024-09-30 | Likes: 3,600

ChatGPT cold emails are destroying reply rates. How to spot one:

1. Opens with I hope this message finds you well
2. Has a 3-bullet feature list
3. Ends with Would love to explore synergies
4. Uses words like leverage, robust, cutting-edge

The solution is not a better prompt. It is writing like a human who did research. One specific observation beats five generic benefits.

Post 4 | Date: 2024-08-25 | Likes: 2,900

The only 4 reasons someone buys anything:

1. Make money
2. Save money
3. Save time
4. Avoid risk

Every cold email that does not map to one of these in the first sentence is wasted. Stop describing features. Start describing outcomes.

Post 5 | Date: 2024-07-28 | Likes: 3,100

Most SDR coaching is making reps worse.

Add more personalization - without teaching what personalization means.
Follow up more - without teaching what a good follow-up looks like.

The best reps understand people. They know prospects are not busy - they are skeptical. They know no usually means not yet.

Teach reps to think. The tactics follow.
