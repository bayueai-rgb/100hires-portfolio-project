3 cold calls I made this week that booked meetings. Exactly what I said:

[He said yes]

Pattern: honesty + specific metric + low-friction question. No pitch. No features. No synergies.

Why I will be straight with you, this is a cold call works:

1. Disarms the sales guard immediately
2. Signals confidence - you are not hiding anything
3. Creates a micro-pattern interrupt
4. Earns a split second of genuine attention

The prospect has had 1,000 cold calls where the rep pretended it was not one. Be the honest one.
