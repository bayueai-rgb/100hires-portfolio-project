## Post 1: The Reply Method That Doubled Response Rates
*Date: 2026-06-09*
Most cold email advice focuses on the first touch. But the real gold is in the reply handling. At 30MPC, we studied hundreds of top sellers and found: how you reply to "not interested" determines whether you book the meeting or get ghosted.
The Reply Method: 1) Acknowledge – "Totally understand." 2) Pivot to value – "Reason I reached out: we helped [similar company] solve [pain point]." 3) Permission-based re-ask – "Worth 10 min next month, or should I circle back?"
It's not about being pushy. It's about staying relevant. Top sellers treat every reply as a new opportunity.

## Post 2: The Ledge Technique for Cold Call Objections
*Date: 2026-06-02*
Every cold caller hits objections: "Send me an email," "We have a solution," "Call me next quarter." Most give up or get defensive. We teach The Ledge: agree, then ask a guiding question. Prospect: "Just send me an email." You: "Happy to. Quick question — if you see value in the email, what's the best next step?" It respects their request while keeping the conversation alive.
