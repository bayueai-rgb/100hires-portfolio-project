## Post 1: Why I Ignore 95% of Cold Outreach
*Date: 2026-06-08*
I get dozens of cold DMs weekly. I delete almost all. The 5% that get a reply: 1) Mention something specific (a post I wrote, a podcast I did). 2) Concise – under 100 words. 3) Make it easy – "10-min call Tuesday 2pm?" Most outreach fails because the sender didn't do 5 minutes of homework. Show me you know who I am.

## Post 2: The Problem with "Just Following Up"
*Date: 2026-06-01*
"Just following up" is the laziest phrase in B2B. It adds zero value. Better: "Noticed your team launched [feature]. Curious if [challenge] is on your radar?" or "Quick update: new case sh [similar company]." Every follow-up must earn its place. If you wouldn't say it at a networking event, don't put it in an email.
