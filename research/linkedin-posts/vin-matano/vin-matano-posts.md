Post 2 | Date: 2024-09-14 | Likes: 1,800

Warm outreach converts 5x better than cold outreach.

Step 1: Identify your 50 dream accounts
Step 2: Follow every decision-maker on LinkedIn
Step 3: Comment thoughtfully on their posts for 30 days (no pitch)
Step 4: Share content addressing their specific pain
Step 5: DM referencing a specific interaction you have had

By the time you reach out, it is not cold anymore. The best SDRs do warm outreach that looks cold to everyone else.
