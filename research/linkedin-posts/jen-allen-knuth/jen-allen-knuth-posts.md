## Post 1: Why You Need 3 Champions in Every Enterprise Deal
*Date: 2026-06-07*
Selling into enterprises? One champion isn't enough. Your champion might leave, get overruled, or lose political capital. One resignation = dead deal. Multi-threading is non-negotiable: 1) Map the buying committee early. 2) Build consensus — arm your champion with tailored materials. 3) Validate independently with other contacts. Enterprise sales is a team sport.

## Post 2: The Challenger Approach to Cold Outreach
*Date: 2026-06-05*
Generic outreach teaches nothing. Challenger outreach makes them rethink. Difference: "We help companies improve efficiency by 20%" vs "Most companies in your space overpay for X by 30%. Here's why." The second provokes curiosity positions you as an expert, not a vendor. I've trained teams at GE and IBM on this. Research their industry well enough to find a non-obvious problem.
#challengersale #outbound
