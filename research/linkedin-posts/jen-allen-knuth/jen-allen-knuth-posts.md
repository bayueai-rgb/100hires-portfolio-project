Enterprise cold outreach mistake number 1: emailing one person and waiting.

At any company over 500 employees, 6-10 people are involved in a buying decision.

Multi-threading map:
Level 1: The Champion (day-to-day contact)
Level 2: The Economic Buyer (controls budget)
Level 3: The Blocker (legal, IT, security)
Level 4: The Coach (insider who wants you to win)

Starting multi-thread outreach on day 1 is not aggressive. It is the minimum to be taken seriously.

Post 2 | Date: 2024-08-30 | Likes: 2,800

The Challenger cold email framework:

Open with insight they have not heard: Most RevOps leaders believe their forecast accuracy problem is a data problem. Our research shows it is almost always a process problem.

Reframe their mental model: The reason your Salesforce data is wrong is not because reps do not update it. It is because updating it does not change anything for them.

Connect to result: We helped [Company] hit 87% forecast accuracy in one quarter by changing one workflow.

Ask a question that presupposes the problem exists: Is forecast accuracy something your team is actively fixing this half?

Post 3 | Date: 2024-07-15 | Likes: 2,200

The reason your enterprise deal stalled: you only have one internal champion.

Champions get promoted, go on leave, lose political capital. When your champion disappears, your deal disappears with them.

The fix: build a coalition before you need one. Map every stakeholder in the first discovery call. Get introduced before you need to be.

One champion = deal at risk. A coalition = deal in motion.
