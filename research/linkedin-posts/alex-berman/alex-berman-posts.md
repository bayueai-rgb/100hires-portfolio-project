Post 3 | Date: 2024-08-12 | Likes: 2,100

Subject lines that get opened (from 1M+ sends):

WORST: Quick question / Following up / Introduction

BEST:
- [Their company] + [Your company]
- [Specific metric] for [Their company]
- One word: Churn? / Pipeline? / Q4?

The goal of a subject line is one thing: open. The goal of the email is one thing: reply. They are separate jobs.
