## Post 1: The 3C Cold Email Framework
*Date: 2026-06-08*

I've sent over 1,000,000 cold emails. Here's the exact framework that still works in 2026:

**3C: Compliment → Case Study → CTA**

1. **Compliment** — Find something specific. "Loved your comment on Jason's post about outbound." Not "Great LinkedIn profile."

2. **Case Study** — Drop a relevant result. "We helped [similar company] book 47 meetings last month using this approach."

3. **Clear CTA** — ONE ask. Not "let's chat or maybe I can send you info or what do you think?" Just: "Open to a 15-min call next week?"

Why this works: It respects their time, proves you did homework, and makes saying "yes" easy.

## Post 2: Buying Signals Most SDRs Miss
*Date: 2026-06-01*

Stop guessing who to email. Start watching for ng signals:

1. **Job change** — New VP of Sales? They'll rebuild their stack.
2. **Funding announcement** — Series A/B means budget for tools.
3. **Job posting** — Hiring SDRs? They're scaling outbound.
4. **Tech stack change** — They just added a complementary tool.
5. **LinkedIn activity spike** — Suddenly posting about a problem you solve.

Tools I use: LinkedIn search filters, Crunchbase alerts, and BuiltWith for tech changes.

Once you have the signal → personalized cold email within 48 hours.

Speed + relevance = meetings.
